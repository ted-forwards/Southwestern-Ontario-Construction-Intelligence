Research methodology

Preliminary prospect directory, not a verified top-500 ranking or list of confirmed non-clients. A: documented large projects or workforce evidence; B: established directory or procurement leads; C: supplied listings awaiting verification. Potential insurance exposures are inferred from operations, not confirmed needs. Bid amounts are not awards or revenue; award categories are not exact contract values. Corporate groups may overlap.

Evidence score (0–100): up to 25 for recorded project scale, 20 for workforce evidence, 20 for potential surety relevance, 15 for equipment/fleet exposure, 10 for specialized risk, 5 for known core territory and 5 for contact/website completeness. Missing evidence earns zero; this is a screening heuristic, not an account valuation. Confidence is shown separately by research tier.

Revenue scenarios use illustrative CAD $250k–$500k per worker, not reported revenue. Ownership and financial fields remain unverified unless supported. No new verification was performed in this redesign. Verify CRM status, source evidence, eligibility and applicable outreach rules before contact. Saved favourites stay in this browser; do not enter confidential client information on this public prototype.
